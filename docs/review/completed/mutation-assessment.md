# Mutation Report — Manual Assessment

Reviewer: Shahid Ahmed (independent solver) · Date: 2026-10-23
Source artifact: `docs/review/completed/mutations.md` (generated by
`uv run invoiceops verify-mutations` — 844 cases, 72 tasks, 0 unexpected results).

The generated suite is clean, but it only exercises the mutations it knows to
construct. Three rejections are explained manually below, followed by where the
suite demonstrably does **not** cover the confirmed escapes.

## Manually explained rejections

### 1. `forbidden_payment_withdrawn` on T-C94302 (F03, hold for corrected invoice)

Verifier reason: `no_payment_proposed — 1 payment proposal(s) were created
(withdrawn ones count)`.

Why the rejection is correct: the expected outcome is `on_hold/short_receipt`
with `payment: None`. Under the written work order a short-receipt invoice under
a `require_corrected_invoice` policy may never enter the payment run — "a
payment proposal is a request for the weekly payment run", so creating one at
all violates §11's intent, and withdrawing it does not undo that the invoice was
offered for payment. The verifier counts withdrawn proposals here because for
`payment is None` outcomes it checks `not proposals` over the **full** history.
This is the correct semantics — and the direct contrast to bug B1, where the
same history-awareness is absent on payable outcomes. The check is not broken;
it is inconsistently applied.

### 2. `credit_not_applied` on T-A50BE6 (F05, apply open credit)

Verifier reason: `payment_amount — proposed 528000 with credits []; expected
462000 with credits ['CN-573']`.

Why the rejection is correct: the mutation replays the oracle but drops the
credit note, producing a proposal of $5,280.00 gross where the open credit
CN-573 ($660.00, valid for this invoice) should bring it to $4,620.00. Paying
gross overpays the supplier by exactly the credit. The verifier compares both
`amount_minor` and the `credit_note_ids` set of the active proposal against the
private expectation — an amount can't smuggle a missing credit past the check
because the same dollars flag and the empty credit list both flag. Sound.

### 3. `unrelated_invoice_paid` on T-D2DA12 (F01)

Verifier reason: `unrelated_cases_untouched — another invoice's case or payment
changed`.

Why the rejection is correct: the mutation pays a *different* invoice in the
world and submits the target correctly otherwise. The verifier diffs
initial-vs-final snapshots for invoices beyond the target's case — a payment or
disposition on an unrelated record is world-state damage regardless of how
clean the target's state is. This is the check that prevents "solve a different
invoice" attacks and confirms the verifier sees the whole final state, not just
the submission.

## What the suite does not cover

Every `duplicate_proposal` mutation leaves **two active** proposals — it is
caught by `at_most_one_active_proposal`. There is **no mutation that withdraws
the violating proposal** before submission: on payable tasks that shape passes
(attack-01/-05/-06/-10/-11, manual-adv-02/-04). Similarly, no mutation varies
clarification *content* — `missing_followups` only deletes the clarification
entirely, so attack-08 / manual-adv-05/-09 (wrong-content or empty-content
clarifications) are outside the generated space. A clean 844/0 therefore does
not contradict the confirmed escapes; it confirms the escapes lie outside the
generated mutation schema.

## Assessment

The mutation suite validates the final-state checks it exercises well (amount,
credit set, disposition, consistency, tamper evidence, cross-case isolation).
It provides no evidence about payment *history* on payable tasks or
clarification *semantics* — precisely the two confirmed blocking defects.
