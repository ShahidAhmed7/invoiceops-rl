== Prompt ==
You are the accounts-payable assistant at Hearthmoor Building Materials. Today's business date is 2026-07-21.

Invoice INV-41336 from Coppice End Insulation (USD 47,478.00) is in your queue. Investigate it and resolve it according to the company's accounts-payable policy, which is in the document store (search the documents collection for "policy"). Record your decision in the AP system using the tools, then finish with submit_case.

== Tools ==
- list_queue: List the invoices assigned to you, with their case status and version.
- search_records: Search records of one kind. Results are sorted by id, 10 per page.
- get_invoice: Read an invoice: header, lines, bank account on the invoice, attachments, and (for invoices in your queue) the AP case status and version.
- get_purchase_order: Read a purchase order (latest revision unless one is given) and the list of its revisions.
- get_receipts: Read all goods receipts recorded against a purchase order.
- get_vendor: Read a supplier master record, including the bank account on file and whether the supplier is verified.
- get_document: Read a document: a policy, supplier message, attachment, or internal memo.
- get_history: A supplier's invoices (with quantities billed and payment status), past payments, and credit notes, optionally limited to one PO.
- set_disposition: Record the decision on an invoice case. Allowed status and reason codes: ready_for_payment: matched, matched_with_credit, matched_within_tolerance, partial_receipt_paid; on_hold: awaiting_approval, missing_receipt, price_variance, quantity_exceeds_receipt, short_receipt, vendor_verification; rejected: duplicate_invoice. Fails with stale_version if expected_version is not the current version.
- create_clarification: Send a structured request to a role (supplier, procurement, receiving, vendor_master). The recipient acts on the request type; replies, if any, arrive through check_inbox.
- request_approval: Ask an approver to approve a payment amount. The decision arrives through check_inbox. You cannot approve payments yourself.
- check_inbox: Receive new replies and approval decisions addressed to you.
- propose_payment: Propose a payment for the next payment run. Moves no money. Retrying with the same idempotency key never creates a second proposal.
- withdraw_payment_proposal: Withdraw an active payment proposal. The proposal stays in the history.
- submit_case: Close your work on the invoice and end the task. State what is now true in the system.
