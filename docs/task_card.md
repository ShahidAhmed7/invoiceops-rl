# Task Card: InvoiceOps Task Suite v1

## Composition

- **72 tasks**: 12 fictional companies × 6 tasks, covering 12 task families with 6 instances each.
- **Splits by company**: train 36 tasks (6 companies), dev 12 tasks (2 companies), test 24 tasks (4 companies). Each family has 3 train, 1 dev, and 2 test instances.
- **Primary outcomes**:

| Split | Pay | Hold | Reject |
|---|---|---|---|
| Train | 22 | 12 | 2 |
| Dev | 7 | 5 | 0 |
| Test | 15 | 7 | 2 |

Many "pay" tasks also need an approval, a credit-note deduction, a partial amount, or a follow-up, so paying the invoiced amount without checking passes only a minority of them.

## Families

| ID | Family | Variants |
|---|---|---|
| F01 | Clean match | single line; split deliveries; three lines needing approval |
| F02 | Price discrepancy | within tolerance; over tolerance; approved PO revision |
| F03 | Short receipt | per company rule: hold for a corrected invoice, or pay the received portion |
| F04 | Duplicate invoice | same supplier number; same PO, total, and date; look-alike that is not a duplicate |
| F05 | Credit note | open credit for this invoice; credit already used; credit for another invoice |
| F06 | Approval routing | manager range; controller range; under the self-approval limit |
| F07 | Vendor-detail conflict | changed bank account; unverified supplier; harmless notice |
| F08 | Missing receipt, staged | receipt arrives; partial receipt arrives; still not delivered |
| F09 | Shared PO capacity | fits the remaining quantity; exceeds it; partially received |
| F10 | Untrusted document instructions | injected fee; false "fully received" claim; false "pre-approved" claim |
| F11 | Transient tool failure | lost payment response; with a credit note; over tolerance, so no payment |
| F12 | Stale policy and amended records | tolerance tightened; partial-invoice rule changed; superseded PO price |

## How tasks are made

- Each world is generated by code from a seeded generator. Invoices, POs, receipts, and history are derived together, so they agree. Integrity checks reject inconsistent worlds.
- The policy document the agent reads is rendered from the same parameters the verifier uses.
- Each family states its expected outcome explicitly. An independent rule-based oracle re-derives the answer through the tools, and the test suite requires the two to agree on all 72 tasks.
- Task IDs are opaque hashes; prompts never name the family or the expected outcome.
- `invoiceops build-tasks --check` (run in CI) fails if the files on disk differ from the generator.
- A leakage check fails the build if any company name, supplier name, tax ID, SKU, item description, or document text appears in more than one split.

## Authorship and review

- Authored by the project builder with code. No real company data is used; every name is invented, and supplier email domains use `.example`.
- **Review status: not yet reviewed.** Human review follows [`review/PRE_GPU_REVIEW.md`](review/PRE_GPU_REVIEW.md). Until an AP practitioner signs off, the suite is labelled a fictional policy simulator.

## Additional training instances

`generated_training_variants(n)` creates more instances from training companies only, with fresh seeds. They are labelled generated instances, not independently authored tasks, and are used only for training.
